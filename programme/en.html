Moscow

Space exploration, Moscow panoramas and history underground

25January 2027Monday

Morning

VDNKh: from Soviet ambitions to space exploration

We begin at VDNKh, an exhibition complex dating from 1939, created to showcase the achievements of the Soviet Union. Today, you can also experience its scale on the ice: try skating on the large rink among the pavilions. Then we visit the Museum of Cosmonautics to explore the story of a country reaching for the stars.

Afternoon

Ostankino: a different perspective on Moscow

Discover the Ostankino TV Tower and television centre, another expression of Moscow's impressive scale. We then stop for lunch before continuing the programme.

Evening

Two levels of Moscow: underground and above the city

On a private tour of Bunker-42, we descend into a real nuclear bomb shelter, now a museum, and see Stalin's office. Then we change perspective: dinner at Ruski, a traditional wood-fired Russian oven and panoramic views of the evening capital from a skyscraper.

Moscow

The Kremlin, Yandex and a Russian banya

26January 2027Tuesday

Morning

The Kremlin: Russian history at the heart of the country

With our guide, we explore the Kremlin grounds and cathedrals, then walk through Alexander Garden and Red Square. Places familiar from photographs become part of your own journey.

Afternoon

Moscow today: architecture, flavours and technology

We walk through Zaryadye Park and across its floating bridge, have lunch at Strana, Kotoroy Net restaurant and visit Yandex, Russia's Google. A chance to see a Russian technology company from the inside, beyond the screens through which we use its services.

Evening

Russian banya: an evening just for our group

After a full day, enjoy four hours at a banya reserved exclusively for our group. Steam rituals, a plunge pool, tea and dinner introduce a Russian tradition in which relaxation and unhurried conversation are equally important. You haven't truly been to Russia until you've experienced a Russian banya.

Teriberka → Murmansk

Teriberka, the Barents Sea and the northern lights

28January 2027Thursday

Morning

Teriberka: towards the Barents Sea

From Murmansk, we head to the northern coast and Teriberka, where winter landscapes meet the open sea. The programme includes dog sledding and, for those who wish, a dip in the northern sea.

Afternoon

The Barents Sea: a taste of the North

After lunch, we take a two-hour boat trip. A diver will bring up scallops and sea urchins, adding a culinary discovery to our introduction to the sea. The boat trip is weather-dependent.

Evening

An evening in search of the northern lights

On the way back to Murmansk, we go in search of the northern lights. If the weather and skies are kind, this evening could become one of the most vivid memories of the journey.
